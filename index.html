Igho Godwin is Gothenburg’s destination for sustainable hair, and scalp wellbeing. We believe that exceptional hair care should do more than make hair look beautiful. It should support its health, respect its natural integrity, and consider the world we live in.

We specialise in hair care products, services and treatments designed to deliver maximum benefit to the hair and scalp, with minimum impact on the environment. Our approach is rooted in mindful hairdressing, scalp wellness, and a commitment to make more conscious choices about how we care for our hair and scalp. Because beauty should not come at the expense of health. And caring for your hair and scalp should never mean neglecting the environment. We do not destroy. We maintain.
