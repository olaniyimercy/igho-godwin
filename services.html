Gender Neutral Pricing

We believe great hairdressing has nothing to do with gender. Our pricing is based on the service provided, the time required and the level of expertise involved never whether you are a man or a woman. Every client deserves the same attention to detail, technical precision and considered care. One fair, transparent pricing structure reflects our belief that quality hairdressing should be defined by the work, not the person receiving it.

Booking Policy

Please arrive on time so we can give your appointment the attention it deserves. If you are running late, we will do our best to accommodate you. Arrivals more than 10 minutes late may require rescheduling, depending on the time available. Please note that late arrivals requiring rescheduling may be subject to our cancellation policy.

Cancellation Policy

We understand that plans can change. If you need to cancel or reschedule, please give us at least 24 hours’ notice. Cancellations or rescheduling made less than 24 hours before your appointment will incur a charge of 50% of the booked service price. Failure to attend, or cancellation on the day of your appointment, will incur a charge of 100%.

Environmental Policy

Our approach to hairdressing is guided by a simple principle: the best care for your hair, with greater consideration for the environment. We carefully select products and professional practices that prioritise hair and scalp wellness while reducing unnecessary environmental impact. Wherever possible, we favour organic and responsibly sourced ingredients, cruelty-free products and more environmentally conscious choices — from the treatments we provide to the products we use to maintain our space. For us, mindful hairdressing means making thoughtful choices without compromising on the quality of care you receive.
