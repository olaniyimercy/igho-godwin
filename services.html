Patch Testing

[cite: 8]

All hair colouring services require a skin allergy test at least 48 hours prior to your appointment.[cite: 8] Patch tests must be repeated if you have not had a colour service with us in the last 6 months or have had changes to your health.

Booking & Deposits

[cite: 8]

A 50% deposit is required for all appointments to confirm and secure your booking.[cite: 8] Deposits are deductible from your bill on the day of your service.

We require at least 48 hours notice to reschedule or cancel an appointment without charge.[cite: 8] Any cancellation made within 48 hours will incur forfeiture of the deposit.[cite: 8]

Consultations

[cite: 8]

If you are looking to make a significant change, require colour correction, or are unsure what to book, we recommend a 15-minute consultation with our stylists before your session.

Sustainability

[cite: 8]

We partner with Green Salon Collective to recycle and compost almost all salon waste, including hair, foil, chemicals, and plastics.[cite: 8] A small sustainability contribution of £2 is added to each service bill.

Pricing & Levels

[cite: 8]

Prices vary depending on the level of experience of your stylist.[cite: 8] All prices listed are starting estimates and may vary according to the length, thickness, and time required for your hair.[cite: 8]

Quiet Appointments
