Patch Testing

All hair colouring services require a skin allergy test at least 48 hours prior to your appointment. Patch tests must be repeated if you have not had a colour service with us in the last 6 months or have had changes to your health.

Booking & Deposits

A 50% deposit is required for all appointments to confirm and secure your booking. Deposits are deductible from your bill on the day of your service.

We require at least 48 hours notice to reschedule or cancel an appointment without charge.[cite: 8] Any cancellation made within 48 hours will incur forfeiture of the deposit.

Consultations

If you are looking to make a significant change, require colour correction, or are unsure what to book, we recommend a 15-minute consultation with our stylists before your session.

Sustainability

We partner with Green Salon Collective to recycle and compost almost all salon waste, including hair, foil, chemicals, and plastics. A small sustainability contribution of £2 is added to each service bill.

Pricing & Levels

Prices vary depending on the level of experience of your stylist. All prices listed are starting estimates and may vary according to the length, thickness, and time required for your hair.

Quiet Appointments

If you prefer a silent appointment without chat during your service to relax, work, or unwind, please mention this in your booking notes.

Therapy Room Access

Please note that our treatment and therapy room is situated on a mezzanine floor accessible via stairs. Please reach out beforehand if you have mobility requirements.
